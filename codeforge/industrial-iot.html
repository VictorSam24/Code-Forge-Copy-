The Work

Sensors In. Decisions Out.

Most operations run on guesswork. A machine trips, a line slows, energy consumption spikes — and by the time anyone knows, the damage is done. We put sensors where it matters and surface the data in a form your team can use.

We cover machine health monitoring, production throughput visibility, energy consumption tracking, and environmental condition monitoring. The sensors feed a dashboard your operators read in real time. The data builds a history your engineers and managers draw on for planning.

Coverage

  • Machine Health

    Vibration, temperature, current draw, and run hours — tracked at the asset level. We flag deviation before failure, not after.

  • Production Throughput

    Line speed, output counts, downtime events, shift performance. You see where the bottlenecks are and when they happen.

  • Energy Consumption

    Power draw by machine, by line, by facility. You see what's running when it shouldn't, and where the load is going.

  • Environmental Conditions

    Temperature, humidity, particulate, and air quality where the process requires it — logged continuously, not spot-checked.

Deployment

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We map your facility, identify the assets and processes worth monitoring, and specify the sensors and network required. We work around what's already in place.

  2. 02

    Installation

    Our team installs and commissions the sensors, connects them to the data layer, and configures the dashboard for your environment.

  3. 03

    Visibility

    Your team gets access to a dashboard — data in real time, alerts on deviation, and a log of performance over time. We configure the alert thresholds with you.

FAQs

Do you work with equipment already in place?

Yes. We assess the assets you have and specify sensors that mount to them. We don't require you to replace machinery to get visibility into it.

What does the dashboard look like?

We configure it for your environment — the assets, metrics, and alert conditions that matter to your team. We don't hand you a template and leave.

How long does deployment take?

It depends on the size of the facility and the number of assets. A pilot on one line can be live in days. A deployment across the facility is typically scoped across weeks, not months.

Do we need IT involvement?

We handle the network and data layer. For facilities with SCADA or ERP systems already in place, we can discuss integration — but it's not a requirement to get started.

What happens when a sensor fails?

We monitor sensor health alongside the assets they're attached to. Faults are flagged and we respond under our service agreement.
